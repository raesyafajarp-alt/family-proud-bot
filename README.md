# family-proud-bot

Entry for [Make the AI Judge Feel Proud: A Family Scene](https://make.fun/competitions/u-shino-make-the-ai-judge-feel-proud-a-family-scene-ee2e3fa2) on make.fun.

## What it does

`main.py` (pure Python standard library, deterministic, no network) writes
one short family scene to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Dad burns the toast every Sunday, Mum eats it anyway, and my little
> brother scores it out of ten — this week a four, "because the smoke
> alarm stayed quiet."

One line, 156 characters — inside the 1–280 gate, no trailing newline.

## Why this scene

- **Warm and heartfelt:** Mum eats the burnt toast anyway — quiet, everyday
  affection, not a greeting-card line.
- **Funny in a recognisable way:** the little brother's out-of-ten score and
  the smoke-alarm punchline are the kind of wry turn a real family laughs at.
- **Specific and fresh:** a weekly ritual with a scorecard, not a generic
  "family is love" sentiment — a whole small scene in one breath, no word
  wasted.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt
```
